pencil-voice

A pencil sound that follows the stroke: it is synthesized live from the pen tip's speed, direction, curvature and position, so a fast flick sounds different from a slow loop, a downstroke from an upstroke, and a sharp turn clicks. There are no audio files.

Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page, and scrolling doesn't count, so tap or click anywhere first. Headphones help: the sound pans with the tip.

Sound is off until you tap or click

1. Drawn by scroll

Scroll through this section. Scroll back up and nothing plays: only new ink makes sound.

2. Drawn by dragging

Drag the slider fast and slow. The spiral tightens toward the middle, so the pencil gets lighter.

3. Drawn over time

A scribble written in two seconds, eased like a hand: slow to start, quick through the middle.
